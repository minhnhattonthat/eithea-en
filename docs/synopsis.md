## Setting
High-school student Shou Niimi (found as a baby at Myoujin High School's gate, wrapped in strange leaves), his
classmates and their teacher are pulled into the other world Eithéa after an earthquake at school. Clans (Moonlight,
Fire Dragon, Night Dream) contend around the sacred Ele Tree. Shou turns out to be tied to the Goddess / the Ele Tree.
The party looks for a way home; romance with the heroines, one confesses at the end (one ending per heroine:
Yuka, Hikaru, Akiho, Lou...). Names and voices: `glossary.md`, `docs/style-reference.md`.
