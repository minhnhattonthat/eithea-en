## Notes
- Contractions, short punchy lines; keep the speaker's attitude over literal wording.
- Line breaks: split at natural phrase boundaries; you don't have to mirror the Japanese breaks.
- Ellipsis-heavy Japanese pauses: keep a few "..." but don't pile them up.
